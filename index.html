    About ML Donation Community

    A community built on the belief that every child deserves a better life.

    Our vision

    To give every child a better life. That means a healthy body, an education, enough to eat, clean water and a safe place to call home.

    Our mission

    To connect generous people with orphans and underprivileged children, and to deliver help directly through hospitals, schools, children's homes and community partners.

    • Children first. Every decision starts with what a child needs.
    • Dignity. We help children as the capable, valued people they are.
    • Transparency. Donors can ask how their gift was used, and we answer.
    • Community. Donors, volunteers and partners are one family.

    How we work

    We identify needs

    We work with local hospitals, schools and children's homes to learn what children actually need most.

    We deliver help directly

    Gifts are turned into care, supplies, meals and learning that reach the child, not just the organization.

    We report back

    Donors receive updates on the programs they support, so you can see the difference you make.

    Questions about giving

    Can I choose where my gift goes?

    Yes. Pick a program in the donation form. If you choose General, we use your gift where children need it most.

    Can I give every month?

    Yes. Switch the form to Monthly. Regular gifts help us plan care for children over time.

    How will I know my gift made a difference?

    We share updates on the programs we support. You can also write to us any time at help.mldonation@gmail.com.

    Can I donate toys, books or clothes?

    Yes. Email help.mldonation@gmail.com and we will tell you how to arrange a drop-off.

    How do I ask about receipts or other ways to give?

    Email help.mldonation@gmail.com and our team will help you.
